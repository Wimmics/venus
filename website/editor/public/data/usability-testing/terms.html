Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary and anonymous. During the study, we will collect:

  • Your responses to the pre-test, post-task, and post-test questionnaires.
  • Interaction logs generated while using the editor, including the SPARQL queries and visualization specifications (encodings) that you create or modify.
  • Task execution data, such as task completion time, response times, and information about visualization rendering attempts and errors.

No personally identifiable information will be collected unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, by contacting the researchers via email).

The collected data will be used exclusively for research purposes to evaluate the VENUS framework and to better understand how users interact with the system. The data will be analyzed in an aggregated and anonymous form.

You may withdraw from the study at any time without providing a reason. If you choose to withdraw before submitting the study, any data collected during your participation will be discarded and will not be included in the analysis.

Please note that the study progresses in a forward-only manner. Once you move to the next step, you will not be able to return to previous pages or modify your earlier responses. If you decide not to continue, you may withdraw from the study at any time by closing the page.
