Thank you for participating in this user study. The purpose of this study is to evaluate VENUS, a web-based framework designed to support developers in creating visualizations for exploring knowledge graphs via SPARQL query results.

Specifically, this study aims to:

  • Evaluate whether users can successfully create visualizations using VENUS.
  • Assess the cognitive workload associated with authoring visualizations using VENUS.
  • Evaluate how understandable and controllable the visualization specifications are.

The study is conducted through an automated online questionnaire and consists of the following stages:

  1. Pre-test questionnaire: Questions about your previous experiences with the concepts and technologies related to this study.
  2. Onboarding: A brief introduction to the VENUS terminology and workflow, followed by a guided tour of the interface used during the study.
  3. Visualization authoring tasks: You will complete a series of visualization authoring tasks using VENUS. The tasks gradually increase in difficulty.
  4. Task assessment: After each task, you will be asked to evaluate its perceived cognitive workload.
  5. Post-test questionnaire: You will complete a final questionnaire about the usability of VENUS and will have the opportunity to provide comments and suggestions regarding both the framework and the study.

Throughout the study, you will work with data from the DBpedia knowledge graph.

The study is expected to take approximately 45 minutes to complete. If you have any questions before or after the study, you may contact the experiment organizers at aline.menin@inria.fr.
