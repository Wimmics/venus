Onboarding

The pre-study questionnaire is now complete.

Before beginning the usability tasks, you will go through a short onboarding designed to familiarize you with the VENUS Editor.

The onboarding consists of two parts:

  1. Visualization Concepts
    Learn the basic concepts used in VENUS, including how SPARQL queries, visualization encodings, and visualizations are connected, as well as the notions of marks, visual variables, and annotations needed throughout the study.
  2. Guided Tour
    Explore the editor interface and discover the purpose of its different panels, tools, and features.
Before you begin
  • The guided tour can only be viewed once during the experiment, so please pay attention to the explanations.
  • During the tasks, use the editor as you naturally would.
  • You are encouraged to use the available visualization templates, query examples, and the Encoding Builder whenever they are helpful.
