Creating Visualizations with VENUS

Before exploring the editor, it is helpful to become familiar with a few basic concepts used throughout the study.

The encoding is composed of a few recurring concepts:

  • Data fields are the variables returned by the SPARQL query (or contained in the SPARQL JSON results). They are used to define the marks, channels, and labels of the visualization.
  • Marks are the graphical elements that represent the data, such as bars, nodes, links, or points.
  • Channels map data fields to visual properties of marks, such as position, color, size, or shape.
  • Attributes modify the appearance or behavior of marks without encoding additional data. Examples include labels, opacity, borders, and interaction settings.
